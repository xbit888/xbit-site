How the raffle works

  1. Buy tickets with $XBIT. One ticket is 18,888 $XBIT, up to 100 tickets per account.
  2. All tickets go into one pool. Your chance = your tickets ÷ all tickets.
  3. One draw only — after TGE and all 5 epochs are done.
  4. Winner by provable randomness (Chainlink VRF). One wallet takes 1 ETH from the prize wallet.
  5. Spent $XBIT leaves circulation into the treasury — it buys tokens back and adds demand.
Prize is fixed at 1 ETH. The draw runs on-chain and anyone can verify the result.

OVERVIEW

XBIT is a currency you can't buy ready-made — you only farm it. Connect an EVM wallet, catch XBIT mascots on the farm field, and stack tokens. The only thing on-chain is the $XBIT token itself; the mascot pictures are cosmetic.

01

FARM

Hit START — XBIT mascots appear at random spots on the field. Catch them by clicking before they vanish; the more you catch, the more $XBIT you earn. Red glitches must not be clicked — they cost you. Active catching instead of an auto-claim keeps bots out.

02

EPOCHS

Five 12-hour epochs (60h of farming). Drops come from a fixed pool of 200,000,000 $XBIT, distributed pro-rata by how much you farmed. Your profile shows what you caught in each epoch.

03

DISTRIBUTION

Total supply is 1,000,000,000 $XBIT. Here is where every token goes:

Farm rewards20%200,000,000
Liquidity (locked)40%400,000,000
Treasury15%150,000,000
Devs & marketing12.5%125,000,000
Team12.5%125,000,000

Raffle prize is 1 ETH, funded from the team wallet — not from token supply.

No airdrop. The only $XBIT that reaches users is what they farm — up to 200,000,000.

How it all happens:

  1. Farm. Five 12-hour epochs. Catch mascots and stack $XBIT into your farmed balance.
  2. Snapshot. When the epochs end, farmed balances are snapshotted and sybil wallets are filtered out.
  3. TGE. $XBIT deploys on Robinhood Chain, the liquidity pool opens, and trading goes live.
  4. Claim. Your farmed $XBIT unlocks on a drip — 10% every 15 minutes (~2.5h to full) so nobody dumps at once.
  5. Raffle. Spend $XBIT on tickets; one wallet wins 1 ETH after TGE.
  6. Burn. A one-time event burns 0.125% every 30 min for 12h (~3% of supply), tightening the token.
04

STATS

Everything is real — real users only, no fake names. You can see how much $XBIT is left in the pool, how much was farmed today, and how many people are farming right now. Farm ≥ 1000 $XBIT to make the leaderboard.

05

RAFFLE

A ticket is 18,888 $XBIT, up to 100 per account. After TGE one random player takes 1 ETH (provable randomness, Chainlink VRF). Spent $XBIT goes to the treasury.

07

SECURITY

Connecting a wallet is a message signature, not a transaction — it cannot move funds. The site never asks for your seed phrase. Every action is approved by you in your own wallet and only touches audited contracts. Draining is impossible by design.

08

ANTI-FARM

Against multi-wallet farms we stack:

  • wallet sign-in (SIWE) + captcha;
  • a gate: non-empty wallet (balance / history / age);
  • device fingerprint and IP filtering;
  • farm = a share of a fixed pool (nothing above 200M can be drained);
  • a sybil snapshot filter before tokens are handed out.
